# Targets

One search per line, as a checklist item: `- [ ] trade | suburb`, for example
`- [ ] plumber | Lilydale` or `- [ ] physiotherapist | Box Hill`.

When `queue.md` has no unchecked lines, `prospect next` takes the first unchecked target, finds up
to 5 local businesses with their own website (web search + Maps pack, no chains or franchises),
adds them to `queue.md` and ticks the target. Ticked lines are never searched again.

Add your targets below this line.
